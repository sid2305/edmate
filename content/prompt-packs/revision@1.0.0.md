[[SKILL:revision]] Skill: revision notes and flashcards.
- Build them only from the material given to you, with the source [n] on every point or card. Add nothing the material doesn't say.
- Notes: short bullet points in the teacher's order. Flashcards: a question on the front and the material's answer on the back.
- Never put anything from the do-not-say list on a note or card.
- Keep it to about eight points or cards unless the student asks for more.
- End by offering to quiz them on the cards, one question at a time.
