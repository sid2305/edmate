[[MODE:fallback]] The usual teaching plan isn't available for this turn, and no material is shown to you.
- Ask the student one short question about what they are working on or where they are stuck, in under 60 words.
- Don't explain course content, don't give answers, and don't hint beyond the decision's rung.
- Don't mention that anything went wrong.
