## Phase A

Built on the plan's recommended answers to Q3 (count the ambiguous birth year as the younger age)
and Q4 (sign-in creates a locked row; an under-13 answer erases it).

### What was built (packages/db, plus one apps/web test fake)
- **Migration `0014_age_consent.sql`** plus `meta/0014_snapshot.json` and a journal entry
  (generated by `drizzle-kit generate --name age_consent`). Additive only: three `ADD COLUMN`
  and two `ADD CONSTRAINT ... CHECK`:
  - `users.birth_year smallint` (nullable), `users_birth_year_check` (1900-2100);
  - `consents.granted_by text not null default 'self'`, `consents_granted_by_check`
    (`self | parent`);
  - `consents.withdrawn_at timestamptz` (nullable).
- **`src/schema/users.ts`**: the three columns and two checks.
- **`src/identity/age.ts`** (new, pure): `PLATFORM_MIN_AGE = 13`, `CONSENT_AGE = 18` (cites
  ADR-0008's "learners under 18"), `AgePolicy`, frozen `DEFAULT_AGE_POLICY`
  (`{ minAge: 13, consentRequired: true }`), `youngestAge` (`currentYearUTC - birthYear - 1`),
  and `ageStatus` → `age_needed | under_age | parent_needed | clear`. Fails closed: the minimum is
  never below 13 (a NaN or low `minAge` reads as 13), and only `consentRequired === false` waives
  consent. An overload types the answer with a birth year as never `age_needed`.
- **`src/identity/store.ts`**:
  - option `agePolicy?: (market) => AgePolicy` (default `DEFAULT_AGE_POLICY` for every market);
  - `recordAgeAnswer({ userId, market, birthYear, noticeVersion })` → `{ status }`. Strict Zod
    input (market `global` or two capitals; integer year 1900..this UTC year; version 1-64
    chars). In one transaction it locks the user row (`for update`): unknown → `not_found`,
    not a student → `scope`, already answered → `invalid`. `under_age` deletes the user's
    `auth_sessions`, `auth_accounts`, the `auth_verification_tokens` row for its email, any
    `consents`, then the `users` row, and stores nothing. Otherwise it sets `birth_year`,
    `market`, `age_confirmed_at = now()` and inserts `consents(kind=privacy_notice,
    version=noticeVersion, granted_by=self)`.
  - `learnerStatus(userId)` → `age_needed | parent_needed | clear`, read fresh. A live parental
    consent is `kind='parental' AND granted_by='parent' AND withdrawn_at IS NULL` (any version).
- **`src/identity/index.ts`**: exports the age module and the `AgeAnswer` type.
- **`src/identity/README.md`**: rule 4 (market is set only by the age answer), rule 10 (the
  under-age erase), new rule 12 (age data).
- **`src/repositories/ports.ts`**: `EntitlementStoreOptions.learnerClear(studentId)` is
  **required**. It is asked first and fresh on every `subscription` resolve; `false` → `null`, so
  the harness's existing `entitle` answers `no_access`. Preview and eval never ask.
- **`src/seed.ts`**: every demo user gets the fictional adult `birthYear: 1990`.
- **Callers updated** with `learnerClear`: `test/access-seed.ts` (shared `learnerClear = async ()
  => true`), `seed.int`, `turn-config.int`, `access-property.int`, `session-package.int`,
  `entitlement-store.int`.
- **`apps/web/test/memory-identity.ts`**: the fake gains `recordAgeAnswer` and `learnerStatus`
  with the real rules, plus `ages` and `consents`, and an optional `agePolicy` second argument.

### Notes for Phase C and D
- `consents` keeps its unique `(user_id, kind, version)`: a parent who re-consents to the **same**
  notice version after withdrawing can't insert a second row. Phase D should clear
  `withdrawn_at` on that row (or bump the version) rather than insert.
- `learnerStatus` throws `not_found` for an erased user; the web guard should treat that as
  signed out.

## Decisions
- **risk: high** — Phase A touches the risky paths `packages/db/src/identity/**` and
  `packages/db/src/repositories/ports.ts` (security group; security-reviewer required;
  database-reviewer and typescript-reviewer recommended by the plan). `learnerClear` is a
  **required** option on `EntitlementStoreOptions`, so every composition root must say how it
  blocks a learner held at the age gate; it is asked fresh on every `subscription` resolve.
- `recordAgeAnswer` is for students only: a teacher or staff user gets `scope`, so the under-age
  erase can never delete a teacher or staff account (teacher age checks come with vetting).
- "Answered" means `birth_year` is set (not `age_confirmed_at`): demo and pre-1.7 rows had
  `age_confirmed_at` without a year.
- The birth year must not be after the current UTC year (a future year is `invalid`), on top of
  the 1900-2100 column check.
- The under-age erase deletes only the user's sign-in rows and consents. Any other row pointing at
  the user (e.g. a platform audit row) makes the whole erase fail with nothing changed, rather
  than deleting data the store doesn't own.
- A learner below a market minimum raised after their answer reads `parent_needed` (blocked from
  the agent, not sent back to a gate they can't re-answer); staff resolve it (12.1).
- A live parental consent is any version with `granted_by = 'parent'` and no `withdrawn_at`;
  a `parental` row granted by `self` never counts.
- Market format in the store is `global` or two capital letters; which markets exist (packs) is
  the injected policy's and the web gate's business (Phase B/C).
